# Custom wooden double exterior door (~180 × 200 cm, two glazed leaves) in Lithuania: prices, producers, joiner checklist

Method note (important for the report writer): research done 2026-10-11. WebFetch to every Lithuanian producer site tried (gamalangai.lt, megrame.lt, durysnamui.lt, doleta.lt, ekspertai.lt) failed: DNS ENOTFOUND / the agent egress proxy returned 403 to CONNECT. **All findings below come from web-search result snippets, not from reading the pages in full.** Prices are therefore "as indexed by the search engine". Most pages carry no date, and some are cached copies that may be 1–2 years old. Confirm every number on the live page or by asking for a quote. Unless a VAT status is stated, assume retail prices include VAT (21 %), as is usual for Lithuanian consumer pages.

## 1. Price ranges in Lithuania (wood vs PVC/aluminium; single vs double; species; glazing; installation)

### Takeaway
Catalogue-type, insulated, glazed wooden entrance doors (single leaf, ~90–100 × 210 cm) are sold in Lithuania for roughly **€1,100–2,500**. Premium 100 mm or modern models cost **€2,900–3,500**. The only per-m² figure found is **€350/m²** (Zyle Fenster, via an aggregator, unverified). No published price was found for a *custom double* wooden door. By inference, a 180 × 200 cm double, fully glazed, custom wooden door is likely to cost about **€2,500–5,000+ before installation**, depending on species, glazing and hardware. That is roughly 2–3.5× the PVC/aluminium quotes the owner has.

### Inferences
- **Species:** at retail level, oak adds about €100–400 per leaf over pine (durysnamui: pine €1,134–1,347 vs oak €1,180–1,700). Larch and meranti are offered by Megrame and Rasvilita, but no separate larch price was found. Larch and meranti likely sit between pine and oak.
- **Rough budget for the 180 × 200 double glazed door:**
  - At the €350/m² benchmark: 3.6 m² × 350 ≈ **€1,260**. This seems low for a fully custom double door with large glazing and probably reflects a standard leaf.
  - Pricing as roughly two catalogue glazed leaves plus a wider frame (2 × €1,200–2,000) gives **€2,500–4,000**.
  - Oak, 100 mm profiles, laminated triple glazing and a carved sun push it towards **€4,000–5,500+**.
  - Add about €150–400 for installation, and more for a "warm" (three-layer tape) installation and finishing.
- A local joiner (staliaus dirbtuvė) without CE testing may be cheaper than a window/door factory. Such a joiner is unlikely to supply a tested Ud value or an EN 14351-1 declaration.
- Compared with the owner's quotes (PVC €1,100, aluminium €1,400 for a *single* 100 × 215 door), wood costs roughly 2–3× as much for the same opening. A double door costs more than a single in every material.

## 4. Checklist of questions for the joiner (with reference values)

### Takeaway
Ask for specifics in writing, not "warm wooden door". Get the species and laminated-timber build, the profile depth, the glass unit build and Ug, the whole-door Ud, the hardware brands, the finish system and warranty, the installation method, the lead time, and a CE/Declaration of Performance (DoP) to EN 14351-1. Compare the answers with the reference values below.

### Cited Findings (reference values to compare against)
- Profile depths on the Lithuanian market: Euro 78, Euro 94 (4-layer glued timber), Euro 100, and 100 mm leaves/frames. Glazed catalogue doors are typically a 70 mm leaf in a 100 mm frame with triple glazing. — [megrame.lt](https://www.megrame.lt/medines-lauko-durys), [fenestrum.lt](https://www.fenestrum.lt/), [laukodurys.lt GARDA](https://www.laukodurys.lt/garda-stiklintos-siltos-lauko-durys-9d4403)
- Thermal targets: an A++ residential door is commonly cited at Ud ≤ 1.20 W/m²K (A+ 1.30, A 1.40), with air-permeability class 4. Sources conflict, and the actual requirement is set in the building's technical project (STR 2.01.02:2016). Better wooden doors advertise 0.65–0.8 W/m²K. — [pridavimai.lt](https://www.pridavimai.lt/energiniai-reikalavimai-a-ir-ne-tik/), [ekspertai.lt A+ namas](https://www.ekspertai.lt/plastikiniai_langai/straipsniai/a_klases_namas_langams_keliami_reikalavimai), [sa.lt](https://sa.lt/kokie-turi-buti-siuolaikiniai-langai-atitinkantys-a-klases-reikalavimus/), [zylefenster.lt](https://zylefenster.lt/gamyba/medines-lauko-durys/klasikines-medines-lauko-durys/)
- Advice from Lithuanian door sellers: price depends on design, species, thermal and sound insulation, locking system and glass. — [duruplaneta.lt](https://duruplaneta.lt/straipsnis/24-medines-lauko-durys), [medasa.lt – svarbu renkantis lauko duris](https://www.medasa.lt/svarbu/svarbu-renkantis-lauko-duris/)
- Lead times quoted: 4–8 weeks (Megrame), 8–10 weeks (durysnamui), 8–12 weeks (laukodurys), about 2.5 months (Rasvilita). — sources above

### Inferences (recommended checklist; built from the above plus general joinery practice, not from a single source)
1. **Wood and build:** Which species (pine / larch / oak / meranti)? Is it 3- or 4-layer finger-jointed or solid laminated timber (klijuota mediena)? What moisture content? Are the outer lamellae continuous (no finger joints on visible faces)?
2. **Profile:** Frame and leaf thickness (68 / 78 / 94 / 100 mm)? Is the leaf insulated? How many seals (2–3 EPDM)? Is there a weather bar or drip on the bottom rail?
3. **Double-door configuration:** Are both leaves active, or is one active and one passive? How is the passive leaf locked (shoot bolts top and bottom)? Is there a central astragal (mullion-less meeting stile)? Is it inward- or outward-opening?
4. **Glass unit:** Triple or double? Build-up (e.g. 44.2 laminated / 16 Ar / 4 / 16 Ar / 4T)? Warm-edge spacer? Ug value? Is it safety glass on both faces (laminated P2A/P4A outside, tempered inside)? Are glass sizes large enough to need tempered panes?
5. **Decorative sun:** Is it applied to the outer glass surface, sandwiched in the unit, or a structural glazing bar? What adhesive or fixing, and is it UV/weather resistant? Which species and finish match the door? Is the price separate?
6. **Performance:** Whole-door **Ud** (W/m²K) for *this* size and glazing, and whether it is calculated or tested. Air/water/wind classes. Is there a **Declaration of Performance and CE marking to EN 14351-1**? Burglar-resistance class (RC2) if claimed.
7. **Hardware:** Lock brand and type (Winkhaus, KFV, FUHR, GU; 3- or 5-point, hooks vs rollers). Cylinder class. 3D-adjustable hinges (Simonswerk, Dr. Hahn) and how many per leaf (load rating for heavy glazed oak leaves). Handle or pull bar.
8. **Threshold:** Thermally broken aluminium/composite low threshold? Height (accessibility, freezing)? Drainage?
9. **Finish:** Factory spray system (e.g. impregnation, primer, 2–3 topcoats). Total film thickness. Colour or transparent? Warranty on finish vs on construction. Maintenance interval (re-coat every X years, especially the south-facing side).
10. **Installation:** Who installs, and is it included? Warm installation (inner vapour-tight tape, PU foam, outer vapour-open tape)? Anchors or brackets? Sill support? Interior and exterior trims (apvadai) and plaster repairs?
11. **Commercial terms:** Price incl./excl. 21 % VAT. Deposit. Lead time and penalty for delay. Measuring visit. Warranty terms (years, what voids it). Spare parts and after-sales adjustment visit.
12. **Comparison:** Ask for the same spec in pine vs oak, and double vs triple glazing, to see the price deltas.
