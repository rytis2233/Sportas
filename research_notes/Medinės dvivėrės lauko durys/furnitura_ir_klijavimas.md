# Hardware for a handle-less wooden double entrance door (sun-disc pull) and gluing wooden rays onto insulated glass outdoors

Context of the project: a wooden double entrance door, two leaves of about 83 × 191 cm in a 180 × 200 cm opening, in Lithuania. The "handle" is a wooden sun disc, Ø 40–44 cm, split in two halves along the meeting stiles and protruding about 6 cm on both the inside and the outside. Wooden sun rays are glued onto the glass.

Research note: WebFetch could not resolve several key domains in this session (baunetzwissen.de, pilkington.com, glastroesch.com: DNS "ENOTFOUND"). Some findings below therefore rest on search-result extracts of those documents rather than full-text reads. These are marked "(search extract)" where it matters.

## 1. Meeting-stile design and fixing the passive leaf (Stulp / standflügel / užlaida)

### Takeaway
A two-leaf entrance door has two leaves with different jobs:
- **Active leaf (Gangflügel).** Carries the main lock, usually a multipoint lock.
- **Passive leaf (Standflügel).** Is held by top and bottom bolts (Treibriegel or Kantriegel) in its rebate (Falz). These bolts are manual, or automatic (Falztreibriegel or Schaltschloss) so the passive leaf re-locks itself.

The leaves overlap in a rebated meeting joint (Stulp / užlaida). Mainstream automatic locks (GU Secury Automatic-DR) are marketed for both one- and two-leaf doors. The exact passive-leaf hardware has to be taken from the manufacturer's catalogue or planning manual, and I could not get those documents in full.

### Cited Findings
- Two-leaf door and gate closures usually use Treibriegel (espagnolette bolts). Rods drive bolts up and down into keeps. — [Baunetz Wissen: Treibriegel](https://www.baunetzwissen.de/beschlaege/fachwissen/torbeschlaege/treibriegel-150330) (search extract)
- **Falztreibriegel (rebate espagnolette):** opening the door tensions a spring, and on closing a trip latch fires the automatic locking. Baunetz calls it the only Treibriegel type also suitable for fire doors. — [Baunetz Wissen: Treibriegel](https://www.baunetzwissen.de/beschlaege/fachwissen/torbeschlaege/treibriegel-150330) (search extract)
- **Stulpkantenriegel (meeting-edge flush bolt):** when the door is closed it sits in the central rebate area of the passive leaf, which prevents unintended opening of that leaf. — [Baunetz Wissen: Treibriegel](https://www.baunetzwissen.de/beschlaege/fachwissen/torbeschlaege/treibriegel-150330) (search extract)
- A Schaltschloss (switch lock) in the top area of the passive leaf locks the Treibriegel rod automatically once the leaf has finished closing. — [Keller & Kalmbach data sheet](https://www.keller-kalmbach.de/p/E6432489/pdf)
- Panic versions exist: a Panik-Treibriegelschloss locks the passive leaf automatically and is approved to EN 179 / EN 1125, with a 9 mm spindle. — [Keller & Kalmbach data sheet](https://www.keller-kalmbach.de/p/E6428443/pdf). In an ASSA ABLOY system the passive leaf re-locks automatically after opening via push bar or panic bar. The faceplate, rods, rod guides and keeps are ordered separately. — [ASSA ABLOY data sheet 24473 via Glametec](https://www.glametec.ch/files/shop/assa_abloy_msl/PDF-Datenblatt-24473-M.pdf)
- Non-panic option: a passive-leaf set with Kantriegel (manually operated flush bolt) for two-leaf doors up to 3000 mm high. — [Glametec: WSS Standflügel-Set 1 mit Kantriegel](https://www.glametec.ch/wss-standfluegel-set-1-mit-kantriegel-vario-line/p/42135)
- A patent describes a passive-leaf Kantriegel whose drive rod is moved by a toggle lever in the rebate air gap and pushes a bolt into the corner area. — [EP 1231344 A1](https://data.epo.org/gpi/EP1231344A1)
- The GU Automatic-DR (Gretsch-Unitas) is described as suitable for one- and two-leaf house and apartment doors in wood, PVC or metal profiles. It is available in the usual backsets, distances, faceplate and follower versions. — [Baunetz Wissen: Automatische Mehrfachverriegelung](https://www.baunetzwissen.de/beschlaege/tipps/news-produkte/automatische-mehrfachverriegelung-7256415) (search extract)
- KFV (Karl Fliether, Velbert) publishes a catalogue covering multipoint locks, Stulpflügelbeschläge (passive-leaf hardware), electromechanical components and frame keeps. — [KFV catalogue PDF (BSS Belgium)](https://bssbelgium.marcando.be/mediastorage/bssbelgium/FSDocument/A0/418/kfv.pdf)
- **KFV AS 2600:** a key-operated multipoint lock. The main lock has a bolt, and the extra locking points top and bottom each have a hardened round bolt plus a hardened hook bolt. It is operated by two turns of the key.
  - Example variant: hook-bolt distance 92 mm, faceplate length 2170 mm, faceplate shortenable by up to 260 mm at the top and 160 mm at the bottom.
  - Faceplates come in 16, 20 and 24 mm widths.
  - Retailers list it as a single-leaf product. — [Galaxus listing](https://www.galaxus.at/en/s4/product/kfv-mehrpunktverriegelung-as-2600-lock-cylinders-22738243); [eBay listing KFV AS 2600 65/92/10 F20](https://www.ebay.de/p/2173316454) (retailer data, partly AI-extracted according to the seller, so verify against KFV)

### Inferences
- **Lock layout this suggests for the sun-disc door:**
  - Active leaf: an automatic multipoint lock (main lock plus top and bottom hook or latch bolts).
  - Passive leaf: an automatic Falztreibriegel or Schaltschloss Stulp set, so that the passive leaf re-locks itself every time it is closed. Manual flush bolts are cheaper, but someone will eventually leave them open.
  - Both leaves need to be ordered from one manufacturer as a matched set: the active leaf's lock keeps into the passive leaf's faceplate (Stulpschiene).
- **Opening and closing order.** On rebated (overlapping) meeting stiles the passive leaf must close first and open second. The leaves therefore need a closing-sequence selector (Schließfolgeregler) if door closers are fitted. The disc halves, which protrude from the faces at the meeting line, must not block that sequence (see section 3).
- **Sealing between the leaves.** Normal joinery practice is:
  - a rebated or overlapping meeting joint with a continuous gasket in the rebate;
  - a bottom seal or drop seal on both leaves;
  - for an inward-opening door, a weather bar or drip on the outside face of the overlap.
  
  I did not obtain a manufacturer source specifying the gasket profile for wooden double doors (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- Could not retrieve full GU, KFV, Winkhaus, Maco or Fuhr planning manuals for two-leaf doors (KFV Stulpflügel series, GU "Haustürkonzept" PDF found but not read: [GU Haustürkonzept PDF](https://www.g-u.com/fileadmin/user_upload/Downloads/Messen/Fensterbau-Frontale-2024/DE/00334_Das_Haustuerkonzept_09-23_DEU.pdf)). The specific KFV and Fuhr passive-leaf article numbers and any automatic Stulp lock still need verification.
- Whether Winkhaus autoLock AV3 can be used on the active leaf of a two-leaf door with a matching passive-leaf faceplate was not confirmed. The data sheet found describes it for single-leaf doors only.
- No authoritative source found for meeting-stile seal profiles or overlap dimensions for timber double entrance doors (e.g. a Lithuanian or German joinery standard).

## 2. Latching and locking without a lever handle

### Takeaway
"Pull-handle-only" entrance doors (Stoßgriff outside, often also inside) are standard practice in Germany and Austria. They are almost always combined with an automatic self-locking multipoint lock. Outside, the door is opened by:
- key,
- an electric or motor opener (fingerprint, code, app),
- or a day-latch setting (Tagesfalle / Tagesentriegelung).

Inside, a lever handle is the normal release. Without one, the release has to be a thumb-turn cylinder, a key, or a motorised lock with an inside push button.

### Cited Findings
- A Stoßgriff opens the door by pushing and closes it by pulling, unlike a lever that must be pressed down. — [energiesparhaus.at forum thread "Haustür mit Stoßgriff ohne Schlüssel"](https://www.energiesparhaus.at/forum-haustuer-mit-stossgriff-ohne-schluessel/16514); [Strahl Schließtechnik: Stoßgriff](https://www.strahl-schliesstechnik.de/alles-rund-um-den-stossgriff/)
- Forum practice: without an outside lever and without any release on the lock, you need a key, or a key hidden outside for emergencies. — [energiesparhaus.at forum](https://www.energiesparhaus.at/forum-haustuer-mit-stossgriff-ohne-schluessel/16514) (user opinion)
- **Automatic locking on closing:** the locking is triggered when the door is pulled shut. A magnet trigger in the frame part sets the swing bolts in motion. From inside the door can always be opened by the lever; from outside you need a key or an alternative release system. — [deinetuer.de Wiki: Verriegelung Haustür](https://www.deinetuer.de/wiki/verriegelung_haustuer)
- WERU AutoLock has two latch-bolts that engage automatically when the door is pulled shut, so you do not need to lock with a key. — [WERU: Zusatzausstattung Haustüren](https://www.weru.com/de/ausstattung/haustueren)
- **GU Secury Automatic, sequence on closing:** the latch-bolts drop first and release the hook bolts. The hooks engage the frame keeps independently of the bolts and lock upward, which keeps the lock working even if the door sags (burglary resistance). The newer automatic double bolt extends all locking elements automatically after the door closes. Suitably equipped doors are described as VdS Class A. — [Baunetz Wissen: Automatische Mehrfachverriegelung](https://www.baunetzwissen.de/beschlaege/tipps/news-produkte/automatische-mehrfachverriegelung-7256415) (search extract); [GU Secury Automatic catalogue (topf-online mirror)](https://shop.topf-online.de/dasd/pd/media/1dc9d9c8cdec43be77ec8dc40d943f07/C0LMTUC658MQAO7PGM28Q9UCA4/PBBH409S7AC61N9QKV7K8FDA0O/GUSECURY_Automatic_Katalog_DEU.pdf)
- **Day release (Tagesentriegelung):** lets the automatic locking be switched off temporarily, so the door can be opened from outside without a key. — [GU Secury Tagesentriegelung site](https://www.tagesentriegelung.de/de/index.html)
- **Terms are used inconsistently:**
  - One guide says a Tagesfalle holds the latch permanently retracted so the door does not lock on closing, while a Tagesentriegelung also holds the lock bolt back. — [letwork.de: Tagesfalle](https://letwork.de/ratgeber/tueren/tagesfalle-bei-haustueren/)
  - Tuerenwerk documents a "3-fach-Automatikschloss mit Tagesentriegelung" operating manual, and notes in its lock info that the electric opener does not work in automatic mode on its lock with day release. — [Tuerenwerk manual PDF](https://www.tuerenwerk.de/fileadmin/tuerenwerk/downloads/montageanleitungen/3-fach-Automatikschloss__Bedienung_01.pdf); [Tuerenwerk: Info Schlösser](https://www.tuerenwerk.de/informationen/info-schloesser) (search extract)
- **Winkhaus autoLock AV3:** an automatic multipoint lock with two steel swing bolts and 25 mm throw, for timber, PVC and aluminium doors. Probe pins (Taststifte) trigger it when the door is pulled shut, and a mis-operation guard stops the bolts firing unintentionally and damaging the frame. A seller lists a day-latch function that allows keyless opening from outside. — [ZEG data sheet Winkhaus autoLock](https://zeg-holz.de/media/ef/99/0d/1758180008/942WBWHTFL94_WH_MS3SAutom_92_E9402117_E9412116.pdf); [Lockshop Warehouse: Winkhaus AV3](https://www.lockshop-warehouse.co.uk/acatalog/winkhaus-av3-auto-locking-multipoint-lock.html)
- A motorised version exists: Winkhaus "Bluematic EAV3", an automatic multipoint lock with integrated motorised opening. — [Batiproduits: Bluematic EAV3](https://www.batiproduits.com/amp/fiche/produits/systeme-de-verrouillage-multipoints-automatique-a-p90974873.html)
- Users retrofit autoLock AV3 doors with fingerprint readers (KNX forum). — [KNX-User-Forum thread](https://knx-user-forum.de/forum/öffentlicher-bereich/gebäudetechnik-ohne-knx-eib/1588558-haustür-autolock-av3-zu-fingerprint-umrüsten)
- One user reports that timber doors facing south warped enough to impair GU Secury Automatic function; other users report no problems. — [KNX-User-Forum: GU Secury Automatic mit A-Öffner](https://knx-user-forum.de/forum/öffentlicher-bereich/gebäudetechnik-ohne-knx-eib/1111339-gu-secury-automaticmit-a-öffner-erfahrungen-alternativen) (anecdotal)

### Inferences
- **The key point for the sun-disc door:** a door with no lever is held shut by its latch and bolts. Something must therefore retract them on each passage:
  - **(a) Mechanical key or thumb-turn.** A profile cylinder with a thumb-turn (Knaufzylinder) inside and a key outside. On most automatic multipoint locks, turning the key or thumb-turn past the locked position retracts the latch ("Wechselfunktion"), so the door can be pulled open by the disc. This is cheap and needs no power, but it is a two-handed operation for every entry and exit. The cylinder escutcheons also have to sit in the stile beside or under the disc.
  - **(b) Motor or electric opener on the automatic lock** (GU A-Öffner / E-Öffner, Winkhaus Bluematic EAV3, KFV Genius-type motor locks). Opened from outside by fingerprint, code or app, and from inside by a push button. This suits a "no handle at all" aesthetic best. Note that a motor lock needs a cable transfer (Kabelübergang) to the active leaf.
  - **(c) Day-latch mode.** The door is held only by a latch, roller catch or magnet, and the disc acts as a pure pull. Security is reduced in this mode.
- **Magnetic and roller catches** (Rollenschnäpper, Kugelschnäpper, magnetic latch) are not normally relied on alone for an exterior entrance door. They cannot pull a gasketed door tight against its seals, and they give no burglary resistance. Use them only as a day-hold in addition to a proper lock (inference; no manufacturer source retrieved).
- Seasonal warping of a timber leaf is a known nuisance for automatic hook-bolt locks (see the forum report above). Stable laminated (3-layer) stile stock and correct keep adjustment are therefore important.

### Gaps
- Exact behaviour of each lock with a pull-only inside (whether the inside thumb-turn retracts the latch on GU, KFV AS 2600, Winkhaus AV3) was not verified from manufacturer manuals.
- No source found on CE or safety rules for escape (exit) operation of residential entrance doors without a lever in Lithuania. EN 179 / EN 1125 apply to escape doors in non-residential buildings, not normally to single-family houses (not verified for LT).

## 3. Fastening a large wooden pull "handle" (split disc) to the stiles: method, loads, splitting, clash at the meeting stiles

### Takeaway
Commercial pull handles (Stoßgriffe) on timber doors are fixed back-to-back through the leaf with machine screws (M6 or M8) into threaded adapters, fixed at both ends. Typical rated alternating loads are around 100 kg (≈1000 N).

Because the disc is split exactly at the meeting line and protrudes 6 cm, geometry shows the active leaf's half-disc on the side opposite to the swing direction will sweep about 2–3 mm into the passive leaf's half during the first ~4° of opening. The split faces therefore need a gap or bevel. The split also creates a finger-pinch point at hand height.

### Cited Findings
- Pull handles fixed at both ends withstand large forces and suit heavily used doors. With one-end fixing an additional bending moment acts at the fixing, which the screws must carry into the leaf. Bracket (Konsolen) fixing separates the grip from the leaf fixing. Pull handles should be mounted as close as possible to the opening edge. — [Baunetz Wissen: Stoßgriffe](https://www.baunetzwissen.de/beschlaege/fachwissen/tuerbeschlaege/stossgriffe-150264) (search extract)
- **Eurofer back-to-back pull handle pair (Stoßgriffpaar) for glass and wood doors, series ESP/EGL R25/Q25:**
  - pre-drill Ø 14 mm at the fixing centres;
  - M6 or M8 bolts (30/50/70 mm) selected by door thickness, screwed through an adapter;
  - plastic washers under the grip feet;
  - grub screws tightened last. — [Eurofer Montageanleitung ESP R25](https://www.eurofer.de/eurofer-portal/media/downloads/dokumente/eurobat/montageanleitung%20esp%20r25.pdf); [Eurofer EGL Q25](https://www.eurofer.de/eurofer-portal/media/downloads/dokumente/eurobat/montageanleitung%20egl%20q25.pdf)
- HEWI 550.33LT pull handle: for single or back-to-back fixing on timber doors, with 2 fixing points. Its rated alternating load is 100 kg on timber and metal doors, and it keeps a 25 mm safety distance to the closing edge. — [HEWI 550.33LT data sheet](https://catalog.hewi.com/storage/downloads/HEWI_550_33LT_de.pdf) (search extract)
- **EN 1906 (lever handles and knobs, not pull handles):** four categories of use.
  - Axial load test: 300 / 500 / 800 / 1000 N for grades 1–4, with max. 2 mm permanent deformation at 75 mm from the axis.
  - Torque test: 20 / 30 / 40 / 50 Nm, with max. 5 mm deformation at 50 mm.
  - Current edition EN 1906:2012; the 2002 edition was withdrawn in 2010. — [BSI: EN 1906](https://knowledge.bsigroup.com/products/building-hardware-lever-handles-and-knob-furniture-requirements-and-test-methods); [dabag test report PDF](https://bilder02.dabag.ch/documents/44/44_6062_30.pdf) (values from third-party test reports; verify in the standard)

### Inferences
- **Design load.** No European standard dedicated to pull handles was found. Using EN 1906 grade 3–4 axial loads (800–1000 N) and the HEWI 100 kg rating as benchmarks, the active half-disc fixing should be designed for roughly 1 kN pull (and a similar push or abuse load). That covers someone hanging on the door or yanking a door held by stiff seals.
- **Fixing method for the wooden half-discs:**
  - Through-bolt each half-disc pair (inside half plus outside half) back-to-back through the stile with 2–3 M8 stainless bolts per half.
  - Use cross dowel or barrel nuts, or threaded inserts (e.g. steel "Rampa"-type inserts) in the discs.
  - Bolt holes should run through solid stile timber, not through glass or the rebate.
  - Penny or large washers or a steel plate inside a recess spread the load and reduce crushing.
  - Seal the bolt holes (epoxy or sealant) against water entering the stile end grain.
- **Splitting risk:**
  - The half-disc is a semicircle about 20–22 cm in radius. If cut from solid timber, the straight split edge will run either with the grain or across it. A semicircle with grain parallel to the split edge has short-grain at the curved ends, which can split off under pull loads.
  - Laminated (cross-banded) or multi-ply construction, or orienting the grain perpendicular to the split edge with bolts in the long-grain zone, reduces this.
  - Keep bolts at least ~5× bolt diameter (≈40 mm for M8) from edges and ends.
- **Stile width.** A 20–22 cm half-disc is wider than a typical 10–14 cm entrance-door stile. Part of each half-disc will therefore overhang the glass or panel. All fixings must land in the stile (and possibly the lock rail), and the overhang must clear the glass and any glued rays by a few mm. The lock case (typically 65 mm backset plus cylinder) also lives in the active stile at roughly 1050 mm height; the disc bolts must avoid the lock case and multipoint rod.
- **Clash at the meeting stiles (worked geometry, inference):**
  - Take the active leaf, width L = 83 cm, rotating about its hinge.
  - A point on the disc face at depth d on the side the leaf swings toward moves away from the meeting line, so there is no clash on that side.
  - A point at depth d on the opposite side moves toward the passive leaf by a maximum of √(L² + d²) − L. With d = 6 cm that is √(83² + 6²) − 83 ≈ 0.22 cm ≈ 2.2 mm, reached at an opening angle of about atan(6/83) ≈ 4.1°.
  - So the half-disc on the non-swing side (the outside, for an inward-opening door) needs at least ~3 mm clear gap to the passive leaf's half. Alternatively, bevel its split face by about 4–5° (the same reason meeting-stile rebates are bevelled).
  - Add normal joinery tolerance and seasonal swelling, which suggests 4–5 mm or a bevel.
  - The passive leaf must stay closed while the active leaf opens. Opening the passive leaf first will make its half-disc collide with the active leaf's half on the overlapping side.
- **Pinch hazard.** Two flat faces converging at 1–1.2 m height every time the door closes are a finger trap. This is especially a risk for children at the inside half-discs, which close together as the active leaf comes shut. Mitigations:
  - a larger permanent gap of ≥25 mm (HEWI's "safety distance to closing edge" uses 25 mm), which visually splits the sun;
  - a rubber or soft insert on one face;
  - a profiled overlap so the faces never meet head-on.
- **Gripping.** A disc protruding 6 cm with a flat back offers no grip. A finger groove, undercut or chamfered back edge is needed for pulling. Pushing works on the disc face or leaf.

### Gaps
- No European standard specifically for pull handle (Stoßgriff) static strength was found. EN 1906 covers lever and knob furniture only, and EN 179/1125 cover escape devices.
- No published guidance found on finger-trap clearances for manual residential doors. EN 16005 covers powered pedestrian doors and was not checked; the 25 mm figure comes only from the HEWI product note.

## 4. Gluing wooden rays onto glass outdoors (adhesives, Wiener-Sprosse analogue, thermal stress, IGU warranty)

### Takeaway
The closest industrial analogue is the Wiener Sprosse, also called applied glazing bars or SDL (simulated divided lites):
- profiles are bonded inside and outside onto the IGU faces, industrially with acrylic foam tape (3M VHB type);
- often a spacer or "shadow bar" sits inside the cavity behind them so the bars look like real glazing bars;
- industry rules for these exist (German BF-Merkblatt 016/2013).

Glass makers' general advice is: do not paint or stick things onto IGUs without approval, and avoid partial shading, because both cause thermal breakage. Any field-applied element can void the IGU maker's warranty unless approved in writing.

### Cited Findings
**Applied glazing bars (Wiener Sprossen / SDL)**
- Wiener Sprossen are profiles glued onto the glass inside and outside. They are distinguished from decorative bars inside the cavity (Sprossen im Scheibenzwischenraum, SZR) and from true glass-dividing bars. Applied bars only imitate the look of original glass-dividing bars. The Bundesverband Flachglas (BF) Merkblatt 016/2013 covers testing, colour tolerances and execution of decorative and Wiener bars (paid document, €9.50). — [Baunetz Wissen: Beurteilung von Sprossen im SZR (BF 016/2013)](https://www.baunetzwissen.de/glas/tipps/planungshilfen/beurteilung-von-sprossen-im-szr-016-2013-4822931); [Baunetz Wissen: Sprosse glossary](https://www.baunetzwissen.de/glossar/s/sprosse-48669) (search extracts)
- SDL is defined as a single IGU with bars applied externally (and internally). A spacer bar is often placed between the panes to make it look authentic. — [Cardinal Glass glossary: SDL](https://www.cardinalcorp.com/glossary/simulated-divided-lites-sdl/)
- Pella's specification bonds grilles permanently to the interior and exterior glass with VHB acrylic adhesive tape, aligned with an optional spacer between the panes. — [Pella catalogue sections (Detroit HDC submission)](https://detroitmi.gov/sites/detroitmi.localhost/files/hdc-submitted-materials/2021-05/Pella%20Catalog%20-%20window%20sections.pdf)
- Sierra Pacific offers exterior SDL bars as aluminium-clad or all-wood. Kolbe uses extruded aluminium bars outside and unfinished pine inside. — [Sierra Pacific: Grille profiles](https://sierrapacificwindows.com/Design/GrilleProfiles) (search extract)
- In the SDL supply chain, the IGU fabricator fits the internal shadow bar and the glazier or window maker applies the exterior bar. — [Agnora: SDL](https://agnora.com/products-simulated-divided-lites/)

**Tape and adhesives**
- **3M VHB on wood:** porous and fibrous materials such as wood must be sealed (paint, varnish or other hard coating) to give a unified surface. Clean with 50:50 IPA/water. On glass and other siliceous surfaces, the bond durability of pressure-sensitive adhesive can change under high humidity or moisture exposure; 3M addresses this with primers and adhesion promoters. — [3M VHB Surface Preparation Technical Bulletin](https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/66019O/vhb-tm-tape-surface-preparation-technical-bulletin.pdf)
- 3M Tape Primer 94 is an adhesion promoter for VHB on critical substrates including wood, glass and painted surfaces. Surfaces must be clean and dry. — [3M Tape Primer 94 product page](https://www.3m.com/3M/sl_SI/p/d/b40065491/)
- Sikasil WT-40 is a one-component neutral-cure structural silicone for bonding IGUs into window frames, with adhesion to glass, coated metals, wood, PVC and more. — [Sikasil WT-40 data sheet](https://industry.sika.com/content/dam/dms/ch01/0/sikasil-wt-40.pdf)
- Sikasil WT-485 (two-component) requires preliminary tests for adhesion and compatibility and is for experienced users only. — [Sikasil WT-485 data sheet](https://industry.sika.com/dam/dms/ch01/q/sikasil-wt-485.pdf)
- Sika notes that the adhesive and the IGU secondary edge seal affect each other's properties. To prevent damage such as "blind" (fogged) windows, Sika runs compatibility tests, and adhesive selection must consider frame material, profile type, glass supplier and IGU edge seal. — [Sika: Strukturelle Glasverklebung im Fensterbau](https://deu.sika.com/dam/dms/deaddconst01/e/Sika%20Fenestration%20Systems%20-Strukturelle%20Glasverklebung%20im%20Fensterbau.pdf)
- **Research on timber–glass bonds:** bond strength with structural silicones varies with wood species. Epoxy gives stiff, brittle timber–glass joints, while silicone or polyurethane give ductile behaviour. — [Pantaleo et al., "Influence of wood substrate on bonding joint with structural silicone sealants for wood frames" (ResearchGate)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/256972860_Influence_of_wood_substrate_on_bonding_joint_with_structural_silicone_sealants_for_wood_frames_applications) (abstract-level)
- Practitioner advice for wood-to-glass outdoors: use a flexible adhesive with a thick bond line to take up differential movement. — [WOODWEB: Adhering Glass to Wood](https://woodweb.com/knowledge_base/Adhering_Glass_to_Wood.html); [Sawmill Creek: Bonding wood to glass](https://sawmillcreek.org/threads/bonding-wood-to-glass.144784/) (forum)

**Thermal stress, shading and warranty**
- Glas Trösch information sheet: IGUs must not be painted or covered with films (beklebt), and partial shading should be avoided because it causes large temperature differences across the glass. — [Glas Trösch Merkblatt 13: Glasbruch Thermoschock](https://www.glastroesch.com/fileadmin/user_upload/Service/Fachwissen/Merkblaetter/DE/13_Glasbruch_Thermoschock_DE.pdf) (search extract; full PDF not reachable)
- Thermal breakage from uneven heating typically starts at the glass edge. Listed triggers include partially shaded glass and stickers or film on the panes (bird stickers, wrongly applied solar film), plus external shadows (parasols, trees). Dark heat sources such as furniture should be kept at least 30 cm from glazing. — [Haustechnikdialog: Thermischer Glasbruch](https://www.haustechnikdialog.de/SHKwissen/3093/Thermischer-Glasbruch?print=1)
- Baunetz Wissen has a planning guide on thermal loading of glass in windows and façades. — [Baunetz Wissen: Thermische Beanspruchung von Gläsern](https://www.baunetzwissen.de/glas/tipps/planungshilfen/thermische-beanspruchung-von-glaesern-in-fenstern-und-fassaden-7681262) (not read in full)
- **Pilkington ATS-113:** field-applied films or coatings can contribute to glass breakage or seal failure, and absorbing films create critical thermal stress at the edge. **Pilkington ATS-123:** static shadows (e.g. from deep mullions) raise thermal stress, while moving shadows are less critical. — [Pilkington ATS-113](https://www.pilkington.com/-/media/pilkington/site-content/usa/window-manufacturers/technical-bulletins/ats-113---field-applied-plastic-films-and-coatings-on-vision-glass.pdf); [Pilkington ATS-123 Thermal Stress](https://www.pilkington.com:443/-/media/pilkington/site-content/usa/window-manufacturers/technical-bulletins/ats123thermalstress20130114.pdf) (search extracts)
- Viridian: shading covering less than 50% of the glass is a concern, and static shading is worse. — [Viridian TechDirect: Thermal Stress](https://www.viridianglass.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Viridian-TechDirect™-Thermal-Stress-Glass-Strength.pdf) (search extract)
- Glass makers generally do not warrant glass against breakage. Thermal-breakage cover after film application is given (if at all) by the film maker after prior approval. — [3M glass checklist](https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/1705610O/glass-checklist.pdf); [Syracuse Glass: Evaluating thermal stress](https://syracuseglass.com/E-DOCS/Tempered%20Glass/EDOCS/Evaluating%20Thermal%20Stress%20In%20Glass2.pdf) (search extract)

### Inferences
- **Recommended way to attach the rays, by analogy with Wiener Sprossen:**
  - Seal and finish all faces of each ray first (paint or exterior varnish, including the gluing face). This reduces moisture uptake and gives VHB or adhesive a stable surface, as 3M requires for wood.
  - Bond with exterior-grade acrylic foam tape (VHB, ~1–2 mm thick) or a neutral-cure silicone (Sikasil WT-type or similar).
  - Prepare the glass with IPA/water cleaning plus adhesion promoter or primer as the supplier specifies.
  - Avoid rigid adhesives (epoxy, cyanoacrylate, many UV-curing glass adhesives). Wood's cross-grain swelling (several % across the grain, versus near-zero for glass) and outdoor temperature swings of −25 to +60 °C on a dark surface in Lithuania would load a rigid bond line and could chip the glass surface.
- **Keep the rays narrow and long-grain.** A ray glued along its length moves mainly in width and thickness. Narrow rays (≤20–25 mm wide) move little across the bond, while long continuous bond lines along the length see wood longitudinal movement, which is small.
- **Thermal-breakage risk.** Dark wooden rays on the outer pane create static local shading or absorption patterns (the same mechanism as stickers).
  - The risk is greatest when rays touch or cross the glass edge (the edge is the weak, cold, rebate-covered zone) and when the outer pane is annealed float glass.
  - Mitigations: specify the outer pane as toughened (ESG) or heat-strengthened (TVG); keep the rays light-coloured; stop the rays short of the glazing bead or edge zone.
  - This is consistent with Glas Trösch's warning and with ESG's much higher tolerance of temperature differences (ESG figures not retrieved here).
- **Warranty.** Gluing anything onto an IGU face without the IGU maker's written approval is likely to void glass warranty claims, by analogy with film application. The cleanest route is to order the IGU from a Lithuanian or Baltic producer with the ray layout as a factory "Wiener Sprosse" option, or with written approval of the adhesive. That also lets them place a matching shadow spacer in the cavity.
- **Entrance doors use safety glass.** The IGU panes would in any case typically be laminated (VSG) and/or toughened. The outer pane choice should be made together with the ray design.

## 5. Alternatives: wood inside the IGU, or fixing the rays via the frame

### Takeaway
Bars sealed inside the IGU cavity ("Sprossen im Scheibenzwischenraum", "innenliegende Sprossen") are a standard, warranted product. They are usually aluminium or plastic profiles. Their appearance is regulated (BF-Merkblatt 016/2013), and industry discusses rattling as an issue. True solid wood inside a sealed cavity is not standard practice (no source found).

The more robust traditional option is to make the rays structural parts of the leaf (glass-dividing bars or wooden muntins with separate glass pieces), or to fix them to the frame rather than to the glass.

### Cited Findings
- Three categories exist: decorative bars in the cavity (SZR), Wiener Sprossen (applied inside and outside), and glass-dividing bars. BF-Merkblatt 016/2013 covers judging bars in the SZR. — [Baunetz Wissen: Beurteilung von Sprossen im SZR](https://www.baunetzwissen.de/fenster-und-tueren/tipps/planungshilfen/beurteilung-von-sprossen-im-szr-7340367) (search extract)
- Sealed-in grilles (between the panes) are easier to clean but lack the bar's depth. — [Sierra Pacific: Grille profiles](https://sierrapacificwindows.com/Design/GrilleProfiles) (search extract)
- Forum threads on wooden windows discuss rattling bars and spacer "nubs" (Abstandsnoppen) as a fix. — [bau.net forum: Holzfenster mit Sprossen klappern](https://bau.net/forum/fenster-tueren/10207.php) (forum; partly AI-generated summaries, low reliability)
- SDL with no glass-dividing bars avoids the thermal bridges of true divided lites. — [Cardinal Glass glossary: SDL](https://www.cardinalcorp.com/glossary/simulated-divided-lites-sdl/)

### Inferences
- **Option A: factory-applied Wiener Sprosse-style rays.** Wooden rays applied by the window or IGU maker under their own warranty, with a matching shadow spacer in the cavity. This is the best compromise between the glued-on look and a warranted result.
- **Option B: decorative cassette inside the cavity.** Aluminium or painted spacer-type rays sealed inside the IGU. Fully protected from weather and no glued joints, but the rays look flat, and "wood" would only be a wood-effect finish. A radiating fan pattern requires a custom cut-and-joined spacer grid; check with the IGU producer whether non-orthogonal Sprossen layouts are possible.
- **Option C: structural rays.** Real wooden glazing bars radiating from the disc, with separate glass pieces or a single IGU set behind the bars. This means multiple IGUs or shaped glass, more cost, and more edge length.
- **Option D: rays fixed to the frame or disc, not to the glass.** For example, rays cantilevered from the disc and stiles with a few-mm air gap to the glass, fixed only at their ends. No adhesive on the glass. This still casts static shadows, but avoids warranty and bond-failure issues. Long thin cantilevered rays will move seasonally and may need intermediate support.

### Gaps
- No source found confirming whether Lithuanian IGU producers offer non-rectangular (radial) Sprossen layouts inside the cavity or as factory Wiener Sprossen. This needs to be confirmed with local suppliers.
- No data found on solid wood inside a sealed IGU cavity. Presumed not acceptable because of moisture and outgassing, but no source was found.
