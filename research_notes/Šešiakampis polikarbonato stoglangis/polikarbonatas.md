## 3. Orientation: channel direction, UV side, minimum slope (and what it means for triangular faces)

### Takeaway
All sources agree:
- Channels run down the slope (fall line), never across it, so condensate drains out of the bottom.
- The UV-protected (marked) side faces outward.
- Minimum slope is about 5° (some sources say 5–10° or 10 %). The 37.5° faces are far above this.

On a pyramid, each triangle's channels should run along its altitude, from the apex or hips down to the eave.

### Cited Findings
- A Russian installation guide says that in pyramid structures and pitched roofs the stiffening ribs must run strictly top-to-bottom so condensate can escape ("ребра жесткости ... строго сверху вниз для выхода конденсата"). Channels must never run across the slope (snippet-only) — [polyglass.ru – инструкция](https://polyglass.ru/montazh/instruktsiya-po-montazhu-sotovogo-polikarbonata/); [polikarbonat.md](https://polikarbonat.md/montazha-polikarbonata/)
- The Lithuanian guide averto.lt says the stiffening ribs must not be horizontal, because condensate collects between the layers (snippet-only) — [averto.lt](https://averto.lt/lt/averto-blogai/polikarbonatas/polikarbonato-ypatumai/)
- A Polish guide says channels should follow the fall of the roof, with a pitch of at least 10 % (snippet-only) — [budownictwob2b.pl](https://budownictwob2b.pl/dachy/baza-wiedzy/pokrycia-dachowe/52638-poliweglan-komorowy-zasady-prawidlowego-montazu)
- Proplastik (Lithuanian distributor) PDF: minimum roof pitch 5°, UV layer facing outward (snippet-only) — [Proplastik – Kanalinio polikarbonato savybės](https://e.proplastik.lt/media/import/Kanalinio-polikarbonato-savybes-montavimas-prieziura.pdf)
- A US multiwall guide (in a result set of AC Plastics, Amerilux and others) says a roof should have at least a 5–10° slope and never be level; flutes run along the slope (snippet-only, exact source within the set not attributed) — [AC Plastics install guide](https://www.acplasticsinc.com/media/documents/Installation%20Guide.pdf)
- Bending is allowed only along the channels. One Russian source gives a minimum bending radius of 175 × thickness, which is 3.5 m for 20 mm; Titan Sky 20 mm lists 3.6 m (snippet-only) — [polyglass.ru](https://polyglass.ru/montazh/instruktsiya-po-montazhu-sotovogo-polikarbonata/); [spk-stroyplast.ru Titan Sky 20](https://www.spk-stroyplast.ru/katalog/polikarbonat-sotovyij/prozrachnyij-sotovyi-polikarbonat/sotovyij-polikarbonat-polygal-prozrachnyij-kivi-titan-skaj-20-mm.-list-6-m.html)

### Inferences (geometry checked by calculation)
- Each face is a triangle with a 104.5 cm base and 114 cm slant height. The hexagon apothem is 104.5 × √3/2 = 90.5 cm, so the slope is acos(90.5/114) ≈ 37.45° (matches the stated 37.5°). The pyramid height is ≈ 69.3 cm and each hip edge is ≈ 125.4 cm long.
- The fold angle between adjacent faces works out to 35.40° (matches the brief), so the dihedral at a hip is about 144.6°.
- With channels along the altitude, every channel's top end lies on one of the two hip edges, except the middle channel, which reaches the apex. The channels meet the hip cut at about **24.6°** (atan(52.25/114)).
  - The hips are the "upper ends" of the channels and should be treated like a top edge: closed with solid tape.
  - The 104.5 cm base is the only "bottom end": cut square to the channels, it is where vented tape and a drained profile go.
- Cutting layout on a 2100 × 6000 sheet: channels run along the 6000 mm length, so each triangle's 114 cm altitude lies along the sheet length. Rotating a triangle 180° in plane keeps channels parallel to the altitude and keeps the UV side up.
  - Alternating point-up and point-down triangles, a 1140 mm strip of the 2100 mm width fits 3 triangles, needing 2 × 1045 = 2090 mm.
  - All 6 faces therefore fit in about 2.3 m of sheet length, before allowances.
  - Only about 10 mm of width is spare, so any extra size for edge engagement makes this tight. In that case, cut 2 per strip (3 strips, about 3.4 m of sheet).
  - Whether 104.5 × 114 cm is the visible size or the cut size must be clarified.

## 4. Edge sealing: top/bottom tapes, hip (diagonal) edges, U/F profiles and drainage

### Takeaway
Standard practice:
- **Solid (aluminium) tape on the upper channel ends.**
- **Vented (breathable, anti-dust) tape on the lower ends.**
- A U or F end profile over the bottom tape, with drainage (weep) holes.

On a pyramid, the diagonally cut hip edges are the upper ends: tape them solid and cover them with the hip bar or cap. The eave edge gets vented tape and a drained F/U profile.

### Cited Findings
- Russian guide: before installation, seal open channels with a continuous (solid) tape at the top and a perforated tape at the bottom (snippet-only) — [polyglass.ru](https://polyglass.ru/montazh/instruktsiya-po-montazhu-sotovogo-polikarbonata/)
- US guides: solid aluminium tape on the top/high end and high-quality vent tape on the bottom. Vented tape can go on both ends, but that negates the air insulation (snippet-only) — [AC Plastics guide](https://www.acplasticsinc.com/media/documents/UACI_140008_Multiwall_Polycarbonate_Installation_Guide_HR.PDF); [Amerilux multiwall guide](https://ameriluxinternational.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/pdf-downloads/install-guides/amerilux-multiwall-install-guide.pdf)
- Multifoil (AntiDUST tape maker): close the upper end with AntiDUST sealing tape and the lower end with breather tape. Venting both ends harms insulation, though it is needed for barrel vaults (snippet-only) — [multifoil.com](https://www.multifoil.com/en/tapes-for-building-and-construction/antidust-tape-for-multiwall-sheets/); [antidusttape.com FAQ](https://www.antidusttape.com/faq/)
- The Polygal FAB020 and Polygal user guide (2015) excerpts only say sheet ends are "sealed with masking tape to prevent dust and insects from entering the flutes" (snippet-only) — [Polygal FAB020](https://plaskolite.com/docs/default-source/fab/fab020_pgl_eu.pdf); [Polygal user guide 2015 (TAP Plastics)](https://www.tapplastics.com/image/pdf/polygal_mulitwall-userguide.pdf)
- U-channel weep holes: drill 1/8" (about 3 mm) holes about every 12" (about 300 mm) for condensation drainage. A U profile is needed to close the bottom edge (snippet-only) — [AC Plastics guide](https://www.acplasticsinc.com/media/documents/Installation%20Guide.pdf); [greenhouseandsupply.com](https://www.greenhouseandsupply.com/glazing/twinwall_installation.htm)
- Colorado Polycarbonate: use 1" breather tape on the bottom flutes (snippet-only) — [coloradopolycarbonate.com](https://coloradopolycarbonate.com/colorado-polycarbonate-handling-information/)
- e-plastena warns that without the recommended accessories sheets can leak and dirt and condensate can collect in the channels ("plokštės gali tapti nesandarios, o kanaluose gali pradėti kauptis purvas ir kondensatas") (snippet-only) — [e-plastena article](https://e-plastena.lt/straipsniai/kanalinio-polikarbonato-montavimas-b21.html)
- Birchwood P501 glazing-bar guide: sheets about 10 mm shorter than the bars to fit aluminium U-profile end closures (snippet-only) — [Birchwood P501](https://www.birchwoodtrading.co.uk/SiteDownloads/Rafter%20Glazing%20Bar%20Systems/P501%20Rafter%20Glazing%20Bar%20System/P501%20System%20Installation.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Hips:** each hip cut runs about 24.6° to the channels, so it opens every channel on that side along the full 125 cm.
  - Apply solid aluminium tape folded over both faces; 50–60 mm wide gives about 15–20 mm per face on a 20 mm edge.
  - Cover the joint with a hip cap or pressure profile so the tape is shielded from UV and water.
  - Vented tape on the hips would let warm, moist interior air into the cells from above. That goes against standard "top closed" practice.
- **Eave (base) edges:** vented tape, then the aluminium F20 (or a U20) profile, with 3 mm weep holes at about 300 mm centres. On a 1.045 m edge that is about 3–4 holes.
- **Apex:** the six tips meet in a small area. Sources point to a separate "pinnacle cap" (see section 7) over taped tips.

### Gaps
- No source specifically addresses taping diagonally cut (oblique) flute ends.
- Tape adhesion on the cut faces at a 24.6° angle is not covered, nor is whether a hip profile with an internal gasket can replace tape.

## 5. Thermal expansion, clearances, oversized holes, thermal washers, tightening

### Takeaway
Polycarbonate expands about 0.06–0.07 mm/m·K, which is about 3–3.5 mm per metre over a typical 50 °C service range. Palram recommends doubling that allowance.

For fixings:
- Pre-drill holes 2–3 mm larger than the screw.
- Keep holes at least 30–40 mm from the edge and drill between the internal ribs.
- Use thermal or EPDM washers and do not over-tighten.

### Cited Findings
- Palram SUNLITE: expands about 3 mm per metre of length or width over a service range of about 50 °C. Double this allowance in practice and split it between both sides. Example: for a 1.0 × 1.5 m sheet, 3 mm (width) and 5 mm (length) in theory (snippet-only) — [SUNLITE installation manual (eplastics)](https://www.eplastics.com/pdf/multiwall_Installation_manual.pdf); [SUNLITE installation (varicoltd)](https://www.varicoltd.com/downloads/SUNLITE_Installation(1).pdf)
- The Proplastik PDF cites a linear expansion coefficient of about 65–70 × 10⁻⁶ /K (snippet-only; the summary was ambiguous about whether this figure came from Proplastik or Wikipedia) — [Proplastik PDF](https://e.proplastik.lt/media/import/Kanalinio-polikarbonato-savybes-montavimas-prieziura.pdf); [Wikipedia – Polycarbonate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polycarbonate)
- Polish guide: about 3.5 mm of expansion gap per metre of length or width (snippet-only) — [budownictwob2b.pl](https://budownictwob2b.pl/dachy/baza-wiedzy/pokrycia-dachowe/52638-poliweglan-komorowy-zasady-prawidlowego-montazu)

- Russian sources: panels are fixed with self-tapping screws and thermal washers ("саморезами с термошайбами") (snippet-only) — [plasticvsem.ru](https://www.plasticvsem.ru/install/)

### Inferences
- For a face about 1.05 m wide and 1.14 m tall, over ΔT ≈ 50 °C at 0.065 mm/m·K, movement is about 3.4 mm across the base and about 3.7 mm along the altitude. Palram's "double it" rule gives about 7 mm total per direction, split about 3.5 mm per edge.
- In Lithuania the service range from a −25 °C winter to a sheet surface of about 60–70 °C in summer is wider than 50 °C. Allow at least about 4–5 mm per metre in total. This is an inference, not a sourced figure.
- On a triangle, expansion is radial from the centroid, so all three edges move. Every edge profile or hip cap must let the sheet slide; nothing should be bonded rigidly with silicone.

### Gaps
- The Polygal/Plaskolite-specific coefficient and the recommended hole oversize could not be read from FAB020.
- No source gave guidance for point-fixing near an oblique (hip) edge.

## 6. Edge engagement (bite), support spacing and load capacity for 16–20 mm

### Takeaway
- **Palram:** rebate depth = expansion allowance + edge engagement. Minimum **20 mm** for sheet widths up to 1.0 m and **25–30 mm** for wider sheets.
- **Spans:** no published span or load table for 20 mm sheet was retrievable.
- **16 mm benchmark:** third-party tables give about 0.9–1.15 m support spacing at 160 kg/m² (about 1.6 kN/m²).

Here each face is supported on all three edges and its inscribed circle is only about 67 cm across, so sheet bending is unlikely to govern. The edge supports and fixings will.

### Cited Findings
- **Palram SUNLITE:** "rabbet depth = expansion allowance + edge engagement". Recommended minimum **20 mm (0.8 in)** for widths up to 1.0 m (40 in) and **25–30 mm (1.0–1.2 in)** for wider. This applies to 4-side or 2-side clamped systems; with 2 sides, use the edges parallel to the ribs (snippet-only) — [SUNLITE installation manual](https://www.eplastics.com/pdf/multiwall_Installation_manual.pdf); [SUNLITE technical guide (thepolycarbonatestore.co.uk)](https://www.thepolycarbonatestore.co.uk/skin/frontend/default/default/data_sheets/SUNLITE_En_Technical_Guide_61353_Web_2.pdf)
- Corotherm (Ariel Plastics): each sheet needs at least one vertical rib gripped by the glazing bar on each side, plus a 3 mm expansion allowance (snippet-only) — [Corotherm installation guide](https://www.arielplastics.com/~/media/Ariel-Plastics/Files/Installation-Guides/Corotherm_Installation_Guide.pdf)
- SUNLITE: purlin span is set by the load and deflection characteristics of each sheet. One SUNLITE table equates 120 kg/m² to 24.6 psf; its 20 mm row was not legible in the snippet — [SUNLITE manual](https://www.eplastics.com/pdf/multiwall_Installation_manual.pdf)
- Amerilux publishes loading charts by thickness, including a 20 mm five-wall sheet, but the values (one shows "138 in") were not legible and the load basis was not shown (snippet-only) — [Amerilux multiwall guide](https://ameriluxinternational.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/pdf-downloads/install-guides/amerilux-multiwall-install-guide.pdf)
- belpolik.by table (Belarus): at **160 kg/m²**, 10 mm → **105 / 75 cm** and 16 mm → **115 / 90 cm** (two spacing values per thickness, likely two support directions). No 20 mm row. polikarbonat-rostov.com has a similar table with noticeably different values (snippet-only) — [belpolik.by](https://belpolik.by/articles/obreshyotka-pod-polikarbonat); [polikarbonat-rostov.com](https://polikarbonat-rostov.com/voprosy/kakoj-dolzhen-byt-shag-obreshetki-pod-polikarbonat)
- eplast blog (non-authoritative): do not exceed about 800 mm between purlins in this thickness range; about 600 mm for 16 mm in heavy snow (snippet-only) — [eplast.com](https://www.eplast.com/discover/post/polycarbonate-sheet-width-and-purlin-spacing-guide)
- Lithuanian standards: the snow-load national annex is LST EN 1991-1-3:2004/NA:2012, and an LST EN 1991-1-3:2025 edition is in progress. The sk values were not in the snippets — [projektai.lsd.lt](https://projektai.lsd.lt/lt/standartu/eurokodas-1-poveikiai-konstrukcijoms-1-3-dalis-sniego-apkrovos-1)

### Inferences
- Each triangular face (sides 104.5 / 125.4 / 125.4 cm) has an inscribed radius of about 33.5 cm. The largest unsupported circle is therefore about 67 cm across, much smaller than the 0.9–1.15 m spacings quoted for 16 mm at 1.6 kN/m². A 20 mm sheet supported on all three edges should have a large margin for a Lithuanian design snow load of about 1.2–1.6 kN/m². This needs confirmation against a 20 mm table.
- Each face is 1.045 m wide, which sits at Palram's 1.0 m threshold. Aim for an engagement of **≥ 20–25 mm** of sheet under each hip bar or cap and in the eave profile, **plus** about 3–4 mm of expansion clearance. The rebate or profile depth should then be about 25–30 mm.
- Most F/U profiles for 20 mm sheet have only a 15–20 mm leg. Check the actual leg depth of the e-plastena F20 against this.
- On a 37.5° slope, snow load per horizontal m² is reduced (the EN 1991-1-3 shape coefficient falls from 0.8 above 30°). The sheet also carries the load at an angle. Confirming this belongs to the structural or snow-load researcher.

### Gaps
- A 20 mm span or load table from Plaskolite/Polygal, Palram or Covestro was not retrievable.
- Lithuanian sk values were not confirmed here; another researcher should source them.

## 7. Fixing systems: glazing bars, hips, ridge connectors, apex caps

### Takeaway
Pyramid roof lanterns built from multiwall sheet normally use one of two approaches:
- Aluminium glazing bars on timber or aluminium rafters, with a gasketed base plus a snap or screwed pressure cap. A dedicated **hip bar** goes on each hip, with an end cap at the bottom of each hip and a **pinnacle (apex) cap** where all hips meet.
- Polycarbonate H/U profiles, with "ridge" profiles at the folds.

No adjustable-angle ridge or hip profile for 20 mm sheet was found in Lithuania. The hip fold here (35.4°, equivalent to a gable ridge with about 17.7° per side) needs either a hip glazing bar or a custom-bent aluminium cap over gaskets.

### Cited Findings
- **Alukap-XR 60 mm hip glazing bar** (UK): for glass or polycarbonate hips. For timber-rafter lanterns, use the hip bar on the corner hips and ridge, standard bars for intermediate rafters, and a roof-lantern end cap at each end of the hip bar. For a **pyramid/square lantern use the "pinnacle roof lantern cap"** (snippet-only) — [Clear Amber – Alukap-XR hip bar](https://clearambershop.com/en-us/products/alukap-xr-60mm-hip-bar); [Clear Amber – 6 types of roof lantern](https://clearambershop.com/en-us/blogs/trade-and-diy-blog/6-types-of-roof-lantern-you-can-make-yourself-for-less)
- Commercial fixed pyramid rooflights exist as a product category, for reference — [Glazing Vision – Fixed Pyramid Rooflight](https://glazingvision.com/rooflights/pyramid-rooflights/fixed-pyramid-rooflight/)
- An adjustable plastic "R angle profile" sold as a ridge or corner piece moves from **30° to 150°**. Its listing is inconsistent: it says 16 mm, and also says it fits 8 and 10 mm sheet (snippet-only) — [Greenhouse Megastore – R angle profile](https://www.greenhousemegastore.com/products/plastic-r-angle-profile)
- Fixed-angle 16 mm ridge or corner connectors and 16 mm base/cap/U kits exist (PCS Plastics, Plastech); no adjustable 20 mm version was found (snippet-only) — [PCS Plastics connectors](https://pcsplastics.com/poly-channel/); [Plastech 16 mm systems](https://store.plastechonline.com/products/polycarbonate-multiwall-panels-and-systems-16mm-c-w-pc-or-aluminum-profiles)
- Polish guide: system H, U and ridge profiles improve durability and tightness. Joining with upper and lower aluminium profiles and gaskets is a practical method (snippet-only) — [budownictwob2b.pl](https://budownictwob2b.pl/dachy/baza-wiedzy/pokrycia-dachowe/52638-poliweglan-komorowy-zasady-prawidlowego-montazu); [tech-plast.eu](https://tech-plast.eu/poliweglan/instrukcja-montazu-poliweglanu-komorowego/)
- Lithuanian practice: channel sheets are laid on a prepared frame with a U-shaped profile fixed to it (snippet-only). Retailers also sell EPDM sealing profiles for frame contact and screws with washers — [dssolutions.lt](https://dssolutions.lt/statyba/kaip-montuojamas-kanalinis-polikarbonatas/); [hausita.lt](https://hausita.lt/polikarbonato-montavimas/)
- Russian guide: joints are made with connecting profiles; thermal gaps are left at every joint including the ridge (snippet-only) — [polyglass.ru](https://polyglass.ru/montazh/instruktsiya-po-montazhu-sotovogo-polikarbonata/)
- A metal "pyramidal hip roof peak cap" (4-face) is sold for hip roofs. It is an example of an apex cap, not a polycarbonate product — [riooutdoors.com](https://riooutdoors.com/product/pyramidal-hip-roof-peak-cap/)

### Inferences
- A practical DIY detail for this skylight:
  - Six timber or aluminium hip rafters.
  - EPDM gasket tape or strips on the rafter top faces, under each sheet edge.
  - A continuous aluminium hip cap over each hip, bent to the 35.4° fold, or a hip glazing bar. Fix it to the rafter through the 3–4 mm gap between sheets with screws and EPDM washers, so the sheets are clamped, not drilled. This keeps holes out of the sheet entirely and allows expansion.
  - A hexagonal apex cap, made up or bought as a lantern "pinnacle" cap.
  - An aluminium F/U profile on each eave with weep holes.
- A rafter top face must carry about 20–25 mm bite for each of two sheets plus the gap, so it needs to be about 50–60 mm wide. That matches the 60 mm Alukap-XR hip bar width.
- e-plastena's W ridge (6 mm) and H profiles are fixed-geometry and thickness-specific, so they will not fit a 20 mm sheet at a 35.4° fold.

- The 30–150° R profile's suitability for 20 mm sheet is unverified.

## 8. Cutting, cleaning chips out of channels, protective film

### Takeaway
- Cut 20 mm multiwall with a circular saw fitted with a fine-tooth blade (about 60+ teeth on 185 mm), or a jigsaw with a fine metal blade (10–18 TPI) and the orbital action off. Support the sheet close to the cut.
- Blow or vacuum swarf out of the channels and seal the ends promptly.
- Keep the film on while handling and cutting. Peel about 50 mm back for taping, and remove the rest right after installation, before sun exposure bakes it on.

### Cited Findings
- Circular saw: fine-tooth blade, 60 or more teeth for a 185 mm blade; standard wood blades chip the edge. Moderate speed and steady feed: melted strings or a gummy edge mean the feed is too slow or the blade is dull. Reduce speed to 2500–3000 rpm for thick sheet (snippet-only; summarised from a result set of cutting guides) — [365plastics – cutting multiwall](https://365plastics.ie/blog/post/how_to_cut_multiwall_polycarbonate_sheet/); [kastlite.com](https://kastlite.com/blogs/blog/how-to-cut-polycarbonate-sheets-tools-blades-and-step-by-step-methods); [gcrystal-pc.com](https://gcrystal-pc.com/en/how-to-cut-polycarbonate-sheets/)
- Jigsaw: fine metal-cutting blade, T-shank, 10–18 TPI, orbital action off (orbit lifts the blade and chips the top face) (snippet-only, same result set) — [kastlite.com](https://kastlite.com/blogs/blog/how-to-cut-polycarbonate-sheets-tools-blades-and-step-by-step-methods); [eplast.com cutting](https://www.eplast.com/discover/post/how-to-cut-polycarbonate)
- One guide in the result set warns that diagonal cuts can crack the sheet between channel walls and advises cutting parallel or perpendicular to the channels. Another says always cut with the flutes parallel to the blade. Source attribution within the set is unclear, and this is directly relevant to the hip cuts — [gcrystal-pc.com](https://gcrystal-pc.com/en/how-to-cut-polycarbonate-sheets/); [365plastics](https://365plastics.ie/blog/post/cutting-polycarbonate-sheets/)
- After cutting, use compressed air or a vacuum to remove swarf from the channels, then seal open ends with anti-dust or aluminium tape (snippet-only) — [365plastics](https://365plastics.ie/blog/post/how_to_cut_multiwall_polycarbonate_sheet/); [Clear Amber – cutting Axiome](https://www.clearamber.com/news/131/How-to-Cut-Axiome-Polycarbonate-Roofing-Sheets.html)
- Polish guides: fine-tooth circular saw or hand saw held at a shallow angle, sheet supported near the blade; clean fine particles off the surface and out of the channels afterwards (snippet-only) — [sklep-etworzywa.pl](https://www.sklep-etworzywa.pl/podstrona/jak-ciac-poliweglan-komorowy); [budownictwob2b.pl](https://budownictwob2b.pl/dachy/baza-wiedzy/pokrycia-dachowe/52638-poliweglan-komorowy-zasady-prawidlowego-montazu)
- Film (Polygal user guide 2015 and other guides):
  - Peel back about 2 in (50 mm) at the top and bottom edges for taping, and remove the rest when installation is complete.
  - Storing sheets in sunlight bakes the PE film on so it "WILL NOT BE ABLE TO BE REMOVED".
  - Mark cut lines on the film.

  (snippet-only) — [Polygal user guide 2015](https://www.tapplastics.com/image/pdf/polygal_mulitwall-userguide.pdf); [Clear Amber – cutting Axiome](https://www.clearamber.com/news/131/How-to-Cut-Axiome-Polycarbonate-Roofing-Sheets.html)
- e-plastena advises not to peel the film at the start of installation, because the surface scratches easily. Store sheets indoors so moisture does not collect in the channels (snippet-only) — [e-plastena article](https://e-plastena.lt/straipsniai/kanalinio-polikarbonato-montavimas-b21.html)
- Hausita (LT) describes removing the film before lifting sheets onto the roof. This conflicts with the "remove after installation" advice (snippet-only) — [hausita.lt](https://hausita.lt/polikarbonato-montavimas/)

### Inferences
- The 24.6° hip cuts are unavoidably diagonal to the channels. Use a guided circular saw (track or straightedge) with a fine blade, support both sides of the cut line, feed steadily, and leave a little extra to trim.
- Use a circular saw rather than a jigsaw for 20 mm material. A jigsaw blade flexes more in 20 mm and leaves a less square edge. This is an inference.

### Gaps
- No manufacturer-specific blade data (teeth, rpm) for 20 mm multiwall was retrievable.

## 9. Compatible sealants and gaskets

### Takeaway
- Use **neutral-cure (alkoxy or oxime) silicone** only. Products cited for polycarbonate include Securbond LMN and Sikasil-N Plus.
- Avoid **acetoxy (vinegar-smell) silicone**, solvent-based sealants and aggressive solvents (acetone, strong alcohols), which cause environmental stress cracking.
- For gaskets, EPDM is the usual choice. Specific evidence on plasticised PVC gaskets was not found.

### Cited Findings
- Many polycarbonate manufacturers recommend neutral-cure silicones to avoid stress cracking. Neutral-cure (oxime) silicones are generally safe with Lexan and other polycarbonates (snippet-only) — [thelexan.com – Lexan caulk](https://thelexan.com/lexan-caulk/); [engineerfix.com](https://engineerfix.com/what-sealant-is-safe-for-polycarbonate/)
- Acetoxy silicones release acetic acid while curing, which can cause crazing, cracking and clouding. The core problem is environmental stress cracking from certain chemicals (snippet-only) — [engineerfix.com](https://engineerfix.com/what-sealant-is-safe-for-polycarbonate/); [KINGDELI acetoxy guide](https://www.kingdelisealant.com/guides/professional-acetoxy-silicone-sealant-guide/)
- Plasticizer migration can cause poor adhesion on plastics. If acetoxy must be used, a silane primer and a test piece are suggested (snippet-only) — [KINGDELI acetoxy guide](https://www.kingdelisealant.com/guides/professional-acetoxy-silicone-sealant-guide/)
- Securbond LMN (neutral cure) is marketed as suitable for Axiome multiwall polycarbonate, and Sikasil-N Plus as a polycarbonate sealant. SikaSeal claims compatibility with plastics prone to stress cracking (PC, PMMA). These are vendor claims — [Clear Amber – Securbond](https://clearambershop.com/en-us/products/securbond-silicone-sealant); [polycarbonatestore – Sikasil-N Plus](https://www.polycarbonatestore.com/products/sikasil-n-plus-silicone-sealant); [Sika – SikaSeal](https://www.sika.com/en/brands/sikaseal.html)
- Aggressive solvents such as acetone or strong alcohols can induce stress cracking. Tape the channel ends with breathable tape before fitting end caps (snippet-only) — [polycarbonate-panels.com](https://polycarbonate-panels.com/en/sealing-polycarbonate-panels-for-weatherproofing-guide); [rescreeningmasters.com](https://rescreeningmasters.com/silicone-sealant-polycarbonate-roofing-practical/)
- Lithuanian retailers sell EPDM sealing profiles for frame contact (snippet-only) — [hausita.lt](https://hausita.lt/polikarbonato-montavimas/); [heliopolis.lt](https://heliopolis.lt/produktas/kanalinio-polikarbonato-montavimo-priedai/)

### Inferences
- At the hips and apex, silicone should only fill or weatherproof the cap-to-cap joints. It should not bond the sheet rigidly to the frame, since the sheet must move (see section 5).
- For cleaning, use mild soap and water, not acetone or solvent cleaners.

### Gaps
- No source directly assessed PVC gasket or plasticizer compatibility with polycarbonate. Plaskolite's FAB020 and Polygal's FAQ (tec437) likely list incompatible materials, but neither could be read: [Polygal multiwall FAQ](https://plaskolite.com/docs/default-source/tec/tec437_pg_mw_faq.pdf).

## 10. Condensation in multiwall sheets and thermal performance of 20 mm

### Takeaway
Some condensation inside the channels is normal. Water vapour gets in through the edges and even through the sheet walls, which are semi-permeable. The standard control is:
- Channels running down the slope.
- The top closed with solid tape.
- The bottom vented with breather tape over a drained profile.
- Clean, dry channels before sealing, and indoor storage before installation.

20 mm Polygal-type sheet gives U ≈ 1.9 W/m²K (R ≈ 0.52 m²K/W). That is far better than single glazing, but its inner surface is still cold enough to fog in a humid room.

### Cited Findings
- Multifoil:
  - Condensation forms naturally when humid air meets cooler surfaces inside the flutes.
  - Sealing both ends was tried and did not prevent condensate, because the sheet surface behaves like a semi-permeable membrane.
  - Standard sloping-roof practice is AntiDUST sealing tape on top and breather tape at the bottom; venting both ends reduces insulation.

  (snippet-only) — [antidusttape.com FAQ](https://www.antidusttape.com/faq/); [multifoil.com](https://www.multifoil.com/en/tapes-for-building-and-construction/antidust-tape-for-multiwall-sheets/)
- Clear Amber says taping does not reduce the risk of condensation. This conflicts with the vent-tape vendors, but the two can be reconciled: tape does not stop condensation forming, but vented tape lets it drain (snippet-only) — [Clear Amber – how to tape](https://www.clearamber.com/news/128/How-to-Tape-Polycarbonate-Roofing-Sheets.html)
- Channels must run vertically or down the slope; horizontal channels trap water and the cells cloud (snippet-only) — [polikarbonat.md](https://polikarbonat.md/montazha-polikarbonata/); [averto.lt](https://averto.lt/lt/averto-blogai/polikarbonatas/polikarbonato-ypatumai/)
- Store sheets indoors so moisture does not collect in the channels (snippet-only) — [e-plastena article](https://e-plastena.lt/straipsniai/kanalinio-polikarbonato-montavimas-b21.html)
- Thermal values (snippet-only):
  - Thermogal 20 mm: U = 1.9 W/m²K (ASTM C177) — [Plaskolite bro101](https://plaskolite.com/docs/default-source/bro/bro101_pgl_multiwall_guide.pdf)
  - Titan Sky 20 mm: R ≈ 0.521 m²K/W, which is U ≈ 1.92 — [polygalvostok.ru](https://polygalvostok.ru/sotovyj-polikarbonat/standart-gost/)
  - Palram 20 mm: U = 1.85 — [Palram SUNLITE leaflet](https://www.specifiedby.com/palram/sunlite-multiwall-polycarbonate-sheet-roof-panels/sunlite-leaflet_2af5785c.pdf)
  - Plaskolite Thermogal 32 mm: U = 1.3 (or 1.07 for "Super", per EN 16153). This shows how test standards differ — [Thermogal 32 mm datasheet](https://ombar.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Thermogal-32-mm-2023.pdf); [Thermogal Super 32](https://ombar.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Thermogal-Super-32-280223-1.pdf)

### Inferences
- With U ≈ 1.9 W/m²K, about 20 °C inside and −10 °C outside, the inner surface sits around 13 °C (Rsi ≈ 0.13 m²K/W). Condensation on the room-side face can then start at about 55–60 % indoor RH. Ventilation or extraction under the skylight matters if it is over a humid room. This is an estimate, not a sourced figure.
- In this pyramid, all channels drain to the six eave edges. Each eave needs vented tape plus a profile with weep holes. Any taping or cap detail at the hips must not trap water at the upper channel ends.
- Make sure the channels are dry when the hip tape goes on: cut and tape on a dry day and store the sheet indoors.

### Gaps
- No condensation guidance specific to small inclined skylights, or to triangular sheets whose channels end along oblique edges, was found.
- The U-value of the exact e-plastena sheet could not be confirmed.
